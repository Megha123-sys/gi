About Me

A journey of resilience, ambition, and growth

My journey in marketing began with a simple belief: every brand has a story worth telling, and every customer has a problem worth solving. Coming from Delhi, I've always been inspired by the entrepreneurial spirit of this vibrant city.

What started as curiosity about consumer behavior evolved into a passion for performance marketing. Through countless campaigns, late-night optimizations, and data deep-dives, I've learned that success in marketing isn't just about creativity—it's about resilience, continuous learning, and the ambition to push boundaries.

Today, I help brands navigate the complex digital landscape with data-driven strategies that deliver real results. Every campaign is an opportunity to grow, not just for my clients, but for myself as a marketer and strategist.

When I'm not optimizing ad campaigns, you'll find me exploring Delhi's food scene, reading about emerging marketing trends, or mentoring aspiring marketers who share the same passion for growth and innovation.
