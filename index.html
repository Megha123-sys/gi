About Me

My journey of resilience, ambition, and marketing growth

My journey in marketing began with a simple belief: every brand has a unique story waiting to be told. Growing up in Delhi, I witnessed firsthand how the right message could transform businesses and lives.

What started as curiosity about consumer behavior evolved into a passion for performance marketing. Through countless late nights analyzing data, testing campaigns, and learning from failures, I discovered that success in marketing isn't just about creativity—it's about resilience, continuous learning, and the ambition to push boundaries.

Over the years, I've had the privilege of working with startups and established brands, helping them navigate the digital landscape. Each campaign taught me something new, each challenge made me stronger, and each success reinforced my belief that data-driven marketing can create meaningful connections between brands and people.

Today, I channel this experience and passion into helping businesses achieve their goals through strategic, performance-focused marketing that delivers real results.
